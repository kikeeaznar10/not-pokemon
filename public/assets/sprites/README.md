# assets/sprites — flexymon, the player, NPCs, items and the car (PNG)

Drawn by `npm run art` (`tools/art/`): `player.png` and every `npcs/<name>.png` are 64 × 128 walk sheets (2 columns
stand / stride × 4 rows down / left / right / up, 32 × 32 frames drawn at 16 × 16 and scaled ×2), flexymon front /
back 64 × 64 (drawn at 32 × 32, scaled ×2) and icons 16 × 16, items 16 × 16, `car/audi.png` 96 × 40 (side view, nose
right). See `docs/ART.md`.
